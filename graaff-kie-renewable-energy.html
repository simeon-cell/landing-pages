Will solar keep my power on during load shedding?

Yes, with a battery backup system. Your lights, security system and equipment keep running during power cuts, and you do not need a generator.

Does solar pay for itself?

Solar systems keep getting more affordable while electricity keeps getting more expensive. The installation cost is recouped after a few years.

Can my business claim a tax deduction?

Your business can be eligible for deductions from SARS with a solar system. Mention it when you request your quote.

Do you install on farms and commercial properties?

Yes. We install residential, agricultural and commercial systems, including grid-tied and commercial rooftop PV.

Which brands do you use?

Tier 1 panels, inverters and batteries from brands including Canadian Solar, Jinko Solar, Victron Energy, Deye, Dyness, Freedom Won, Lumax and LuxPower Tek.

Do you look after the system after installation?

Yes. We monitor and maintain the solar systems we install.

Can you help with financing?

Our advice covers the whole project, from financing a solar project to designing the system.

Does the quote cost anything?
